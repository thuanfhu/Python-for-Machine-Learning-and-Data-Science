Because of this, we decided to add this entire section showing you what a realistic data cleaning and formatting job looks like, so keep in mind, you may find this a bit boring (but also extremely informative!). I promise you once we start learning about new algorithms it will get very exciting again. I also want to note that future algorithm projects won't have such intense cleaning and formatting lectures (otherwise the course would be 80% just cleaning and formatting random data sets).

In conclusion, I hope you find this series of lectures as an informative real world example for how to deal with a large data set with lots of different feature types. Enjoy!
